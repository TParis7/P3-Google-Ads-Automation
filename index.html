07b CSV Upload Lessons Learned

Documenting known issues and workarounds for CSV bulk upload to save time on future campaigns:

IssueWhat HappenedSolution / Workaround
EU Political Ads ColumnCampaigns CSV rejected — missing required "EU political ads" columnAlways include "EU political ads" column with value "No" in Campaigns CSV.
Negative Keywords: Ad Group Level OnlyWeb UI bulk upload does not support campaign-level negative keywordsAssign every negative keyword to specific ad groups. To cover a whole campaign, duplicate each negative across all ad groups in that campaign.
Policy Violations on Negatives5 terms triggered policy violations even as negatives: "sugar daddy", "sugar mama", "payday loan", "credit repair", "dating app"Remove all policy-violating terms before upload. Google scans negative keyword text against content policies.
Ad Group Name MismatchNegative keywords CSV used wrong ad group names (e.g., "Donation Appeals" instead of "Donate General")Always verify ad group names match exactly. Use 02_Ad_Groups.csv as source of truth.
Sitelinks / Callouts: No CSVNo CSV template exists. "Asset action" column values "Add" and "Create" both rejected.Add manually via Asset Studio (Assets → Assets → + → Sitelink/Callout). No bulk upload possible.
Image Assets: Not for SearchImage option doesn't appear in asset dropdown for Search campaignsImage extensions are only for Display/Performance Max campaigns. Ad Grant accounts are Search-only — no image assets available.
Business Logo Upload Error"An error occurred. Please try again later" when uploading logoEnsure square (1:1), min 128×128px, PNG with transparent background, under 5MB. May also be a temporary Google error — retry after a few minutes.
RSA "Exceeds Limit""The number of Ad exceeds a limit" error on RSA uploadThis means RSAs already exist from a prior upload — not an actual error. Do not re-upload.
Cascading "An error occurred"All rows show "An error occurred" — server-side cascade from policy violationsFix the policy violations first, then retry the entire file. May need to wait a few minutes between attempts.
Low Search Volume13 of 48 keywords rejected for insufficient search volume (too long/specific)Keep keywords to 2-3 words for niche topics. Shorten long-tail phrases (e.g., "STEM mentor for students" → "STEM mentor", "doctors mentoring underserved youth" → "mentoring underserved youth").
Keywords: Use Google TemplateGeneric bulk upload format produced wrong/junk keywords in accountDownload template from Keywords page → "UPLOAD KEYWORDS". Uses "Row Type", "Action", "Phrase match" format. Upload one CSV per campaign, then verify keywords appeared correctly.
Negative Keywords: Manual OnlyCSV bulk upload was unreliable for negative keywordsAdd negative keywords manually in the Google Ads UI. Do not rely on CSV upload for negatives.
